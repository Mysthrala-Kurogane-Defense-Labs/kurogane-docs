# A first preparation session for a small company

## Input

Choose one line, workshop or business-critical workflow. Invite the person responsible for operations and the person who maintains IT/OT. Bring existing inventory and service-provider information if available. Do not start with scanning or purchasing software.

## A bounded 30-minute exercise

1. **Scope, 5 minutes:** name the activity, owner and consequence of losing it. Record what is outside this first review.
2. **Dependencies, 10 minutes:** list the machines, workstations, network equipment, external support and backup needed by that activity. Mark unknown ownership or versions.
3. **Access and recovery, 10 minutes:** identify who can connect remotely, how access is approved, and who has verified a restore. A backup's existence is not a restore result.
4. **Priorities, 5 minutes:** choose three unanswered questions or actions. Assign one owner and a review date to each.

## Output to retain privately

| Field | Example using invented data |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and owner | Synthetic packaging line; operations lead |
| Dependency / evidence | Engineering workstation; dated inventory export |
| Unknown | Remote-support accounts have not been reviewed |
| Next action | Maintenance lead requests the account list by an agreed date |
| Completion evidence | Reviewed account list with access removed or accepted |

Use [plant modeling](plant-modeling.md) to expand the inventory and [operational risk](operational-risk.md) to prioritize. Do not put the completed register into public issues.

## Completion and limits

The session is complete when scope, three priorities, owners and next review are recorded. It is preparation, not a security audit or legal determination. A real assessment needs an agreed scope, permitted methods, deliverables and stop conditions.
