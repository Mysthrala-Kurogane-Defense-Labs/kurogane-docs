# Glossary

- OT: operational technology used to monitor or control physical processes.
- IT: information technology systems used for business and digital operations.
- PLC: programmable logic controller.
- SCADA: supervisory control and data acquisition.
- Digital twin: a useful model of assets, signals, relationships, and operating state.
- Satellite architecture: local integration layer intended to support visibility while limiting unnecessary exposure.
- Data sovereignty: retaining clear control over where data is processed, stored, and accessed.
