# NIS2: applicability and evidence questions

## Verified reference

[Directive (EU) 2022/2555](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2022-81963), particularly Article 2 and Annexes I–II, defines scope through sectors, size rules and exceptions. A small company is not automatically subject to every NIS2 obligation. Article 21 includes supply-chain security among risk-management measures for covered entities.

Do not equate a customer's supplier questionnaire with direct statutory applicability. Record the customer's contractual requirement separately.

## Review steps

1. Identify the legal entity, activity, size, country and sector evidence.
2. Check the current national implementing law, authority guidance and any exceptions or individual designation. Record the checked date; do not infer current transposition from an old announcement.
3. Separate direct duties from customer contracts and useful voluntary practices.
4. For each relevant requirement, map an owner, action, dated evidence and unresolved gap.

## Example evidence fields

Requirement/source → entity and scope → owner → inventory/access/recovery evidence → date → reviewer → next action. Keep the result private and have applicability reviewed by a qualified legal or compliance professional.

## Limit

No repository, software feature or checklist grants NIS2 compliance. Public SDK and lab tests are synthetic technical evidence only. The [reference register](references.md) records the checked sources and distinguishes final guidance from drafts.
